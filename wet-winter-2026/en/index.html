Dear friends,

we are excited to announce the Winter World Event Tournament in Milan!

It will take place at Casa dei Giochi: Via Sant'Uguzzone n. 8 - Milan.

Useful information

  • The tournament is a 2000-point event.
  • Painting is not required for participation, but regiments must be assembled so that models can be easily distinguished by the opponent.
  • The registration deadline is November 25th, and the registration fee is €25.
  • For those who make the payment by November 15th, the fee will be reduced to €20. Payment is made via PayPal.
  • Please note that the tournament has a maximum of 30 participants. To avoid organizational issues, only players who have completed their payment by November 25th will be considered officially registered.
  • The deadline for submitting army lists, which must be uploaded to Longshanks, is November 28th.
  • For lunch, it will be possible to order sandwiches from the game club’s bar (the cost is not included in the tournament registration fee).
  • It is considered good practice to bring printed copies of your lists, to help your opponent better understand what they’ll be facing.
  • Players are informed that any changes to the rules or factions will only be taken into consideration if they are released by November 9th.

Painting contest

Monster category

For the winter edition, there will also be a painting contest open to everyone: players, companions, pushers, and caregivers alike. Everyone is invited to display and present their favorite model from the Monster category; therefore, it is not necessary for the model to be included in the army list.

The aim of the contest is to introduce even those who are not familiar with competitive play to our hobby. Therefore, if a player coming from out of town wishes to bring models belonging to other members of their club, the organization warmly encourages them to do so.
